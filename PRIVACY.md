# ClipBubble privacy policy

ClipBubble keeps your clipboard history on your own devices. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, so nothing you copy is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

## What ClipBubble stores

ClipBubble saves what you copy (text, images, files, screenshots and downloads on a computer, and what you copy or share on an iPhone) in a database on that device. It deletes items after 7 days. On a computer you can switch off any kind of item in Settings, and deleting the app deletes its history.

## Syncing

When you link your iPhone to a computer, ClipBubble syncs your history directly between your devices over your own network, using encrypted SSH connections. The data travels only between devices you linked yourself.

## iPhone permissions

- **Camera** is used only to scan the pairing code your computer shows. No photo is taken or kept.
- **Local network** is used to reach the computers you linked.
- **Allow Full Access** for the ClipBubble keyboard lets it read the history the app keeps and reach your computers. The keyboard doesn't record what you type.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here. For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/rohit3a/clipbubble/issues.
